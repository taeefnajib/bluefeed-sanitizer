Privacy Policy

The BlueFeed Sanitizer (Chrome extension) · Effective 19 October 2026

Short version: The BlueFeed Sanitizer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your own browser, on your own computer.

What the extension does

The BlueFeed Sanitizer filters your Facebook feed. It hides posts, comments and images that contain keywords you choose, or images that an AI model running on your computer identifies as sexual, drug-related or violent content, according to your settings.

Data processed on your computer only

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • No sending of browsing activity, Facebook content, images or settings to the developer or any third party.
  • No selling or transferring of user data, and no use of data for purposes unrelated to filtering your feed.
  • No use of data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Network requests

The extension makes only one kind of outside request: a one-time download of the AI model files (about 150 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model hosting service. No information about you or your browsing is sent with this request beyond what any browser sends when downloading a file (such as your IP address, which Hugging Face handles under its own privacy policy). After the first download the model is stored on your computer and loaded from there.

Facebook images are loaded from Facebook's own servers (fbcdn.net), just as Facebook itself loads them, so they can be analysed locally.

Deleting your data

You can clear learned exceptions at any time from the extension's popup. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of its stored data, including settings, keyword lists and the saved AI model.

Children

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
